Events

Compete, celebrate, share

Indoor golf is even better in company: weekly leagues, monthly tournaments and private events made to measure.

Night league

Teams of 2–4, one match a week for 8 weeks. Live leaderboard, a different course every round and a grand final with prizes.

  • Thursdays, 8 pm – 11 pm
  • All levels — simulator handicap
  • Members: 50% off · For pricing, contact us

Monthly tournaments

One Saturday a month: 9-hole stroke play plus longest-drive and closest-to-the-pin contests. Gross and handicap prizes.

  • Surprise course every edition
  • Club annual ranking
  • Members: 50% off · For pricing, contact us

Birthdays & celebrations

The club exclusively for your crew: game formats everyone can play, music to your taste and optional catering.

  • Up to 16 guests across 2 bays (rotating play)
  • Optional catering and drinks
  • Any day of the week · For pricing, contact us
