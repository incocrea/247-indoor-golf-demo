Booking & access

Playing & equipment

Full use of the simulator for up to 4 players, and balls. Just bring the will to play and your own clubs — if you need rental clubs, contact us before your booked time. Every bay includes the Uneekor launch monitor, GSPro software with thousands of world-famous courses, driving-range and practice modes, full club and ball data, comfortable seating, premium hitting mats and a climate-controlled environment.

We carry the Uneekor EYE XO2, with improved capabilities and new AI Trainer features for faster improvement.

Yes. Our AI-powered technology analyzes your swing and ball flight in real time, providing instant feedback, slow-motion swing videos and personalized coaching to help you improve faster.

Yes. Club Optix® and Swing Optix® provide instant swing replays, impact analysis and shot data so you can review your mechanics, track progress and share your best swings.

Our simulators feature Uneekor technology paired with GSPro, providing realistic ball flight, advanced practice tools and access to thousands of courses from around the world.

On the simulator, 18 holes take roughly 45–60 minutes per player. For a first visit we recommend one hour per pair of players.

To protect screens and turf: clean your clubs before playing (you're welcome to use our club washing station if you forgot) and wear clean trainers — no metal spikes. For safety, only one active player is allowed on the hitting turf at a time; all other guests must remain behind the designated safety line or netting to avoid high-velocity ricochets or club strikes. Please pack up your gear and step out of the bay exactly when your reservation time expires.

Membership

Memberships provide access to the club, included monthly hours or discounted bay rates (depending on your plan), member-exclusive events and additional perks. Founding Members also receive exclusive benefits and locked-in introductory pricing while continuously active. See the membership tiers.

No long-term contracts. Members pay monthly and can manage their membership according to the membership terms.

Yes — there's no lock-in. To switch tiers or cancel, just write us an email at least 5 business days before your renewal date to avoid charges for the next billing cycle.

Most tiers include free guest passes — 3 per month on Founding, Amateur and Scratch, 4 on Pro. Guests always play within your booking.

Membership pricing may change at any time. If the fee changes, the new rate applies at your next renewal and you will be notified of any such change. The club also reserves the right to cancel or revoke membership privileges at its sole discretion.

The club

We built 24/7 Indoor Golf Club around one goal: helping golfers improve. Unlike traditional indoor golf facilities, we focus on premium technology, measurable progress and a members-first experience. Whether you're practicing before work, playing after dinner or escaping bad weather, our club is designed around your schedule — not ours.

We chose Uneekor because it's one of the most accurate launch monitor systems available. Members receive professional-grade ball and club data: ball speed, club path, face angle, launch angle, spin, carry distance and impact location. Every practice session provides real feedback — not just entertainment.

Because we're more than simulator rentals. Our focus: measurable improvement, a premium member experience, AI-guided practice, 24/7 access, a luxury environment, and community events, leagues and tournaments. Our goal is to become your golf home — not just a place to play.
