FAQs

Answers, before you even ask

Everything about booking, access, playing and membership. Can't find your question? Contact us.

Booking & access

No. Anyone can book a bay by the hour. Membership pays off if you play several times a month: it includes hours, discounts and priority booking.

When your booking is confirmed you receive an email with your digital key. The door unlocks from your phone during your time slot and your bay switches on automatically.

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before, from the link in your confirmation email. With less notice the booking is non-refundable.

Your digital access works only during your slot, and bookings end on time so the next group can start fresh. Arrive a few minutes ahead so you play your full hour.

Playing & equipment

Full use of the simulator for up to 4 players, loaner clubs for right- and left-handed players, and balls. Just bring the will to play — and your own clubs if you prefer.

Absolutely. Game modes and target challenges make the first visit fun from minute one, and we offer intro lessons with a pro to get you started properly.

On the simulator, 9 holes take roughly 45–60 minutes per player. For a first visit we recommend one hour per pair of players.

To protect screens and turf: clean your clubs before playing, wear clean trainers (no metal spikes) and respect your booking's end time.

Membership

Included playing hours each month, reduced member rates, priority booking and guest perks depending on your tier. See the membership page for details.

Yes — there's no lock-in. You can switch tiers or cancel effective the following month, online, no questions asked.

Of course. All tiers get guest discounts, and Fairway and Albatross include free guest passes. Guests always play under your booking.

Events & groups

Yes — for birthdays, get-togethers and company events, any day of the week. See events or business for options and prices.

All year round: a night league every season and one tournament a month, both open to members and non-members.
