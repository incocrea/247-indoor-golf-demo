    How payment works

    Money moves at the club, the system tracks everything

    Members & hour packs first

    Membership fees and hour packs are paid at the club or by Zelle/transfer. Booking simply spends hours you already own.

    Guests pay on arrival

    Guests book instantly and pay at the club. No-show protection: one active booking per guest, email confirmation and a strike policy.

    Door code per booking

    Every confirmed booking generates a temporary access code linked to your time slot — 24/7 access with no staff needed.

    Before you come

    House rules

    Arrive on time

    Your digital access works only during your slot. Arrive a few minutes ahead so you're ready to play from the first minute.

    Clean clubs

    To protect screens and mats, give your clubs a good clean before playing. Spotless loaner clubs are always available.

    24-hour cancellation

    Free cancellation and changes up to 24 hours before, from this page or My account. Included hours go straight back to your balance.

    Playing every week?

    Membership gets you included hours, member rates and priority booking from $29/month.
