The technology

See every number. Fix every swing.

Every bay pairs the Uneekor EYE XO2 launch monitor with GSPro simulation — tour-grade ball and club data, waiting for you at 3 a.m.

01 — Ball & club data

Every shot, measured

Speed, spin, launch and face angle captured the instant the club meets the ball — with high-speed camera images of the impact itself. No guessing about what your ball just did.

  • Launch angle 19.8°
  • Back spin 5856
  • Side angle R 5.4°
  • Side spin R 226
02 — Play anywhere

Thousands of courses, one bay

Play full rounds on the world's most famous courses in GSPro, or work the range in practice mode. Every session is saved shot by shot, so your carry, total and dispersion are always one tap away.

  • Carry 121.8 yd
  • Total 128.0 yd
  • Smash 1.14
  • Ball speed 94.5 mph
03 — AI swing coaching

A coach that never sleeps

Your swing is filmed and scored from address to impact. The system flags what went wrong at each stage — sequence, posture, head position — and shows you, side by side, what the correct move looks like.

  • Swing score 80/100
  • Shoulder turn 92°
  • Club speed 107.7 mph
  • Tempo 0.90 / 0.30 s

More than a place to hit balls

Practice that actually counts

See your swing scored phase by phase, watch the numbers move in the right direction and leave knowing exactly what to work on next. That is the difference between hitting balls and getting better.
