How it works

Book, walk in, play

Everyone is welcome — no membership needed to play. Members get included hours, better rates and priority booking.

1

Book online

Pick a bay, day and time in our booking calendar and pay securely. Bookings run on the Albaplay platform.

2

Walk in with your phone

Your confirmation email carries your digital key: the door unlocks from your phone during your slot — even at 3 a.m.

3

Play & improve

Your bay switches on with your booking. Play legendary courses, take on challenges or train with precise data on every shot.

The technology

Pro-level simulators

Every bay runs a next-generation simulator that measures ball speed, spin, launch angle and flight curve with pinpoint accuracy — so the game feels real and the practice actually counts.

Legendary courses

Play full rounds on 150+ of the world's most famous courses without leaving the city.

Games & challenges

Longest drive, target practice, capture the flag… fun formats for every age and level.

Data-driven practice

Real-time swing metrics so you practise with purpose and see your progress session after session.
